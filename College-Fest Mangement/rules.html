Festival Rules

A few simple things to help everyone have a great time.

Before you take part

  1. Register for each event separately using the registration page.
  2. Bring your college ID and arrive at least 15 minutes before your event.
  3. Read the event details and bring any materials you need.
  4. Follow instructions from the event coordinators and volunteers.

During the festival

  • Be respectful to other participants, volunteers, performers, and guests.
  • Keep the campus clean and take care of event equipment.
  • Do not interfere with another participant's performance or work.
  • The judges' decisions are final. Most importantly, have fun!
